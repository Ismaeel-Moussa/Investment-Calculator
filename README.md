# 📈 Compound Investment Calculator | حاسبة الاستثمار التراكمي

A modern, responsive, and high-performance **Compound Investment & Wealth Calculator** built with **React**, **TypeScript**, **Vite**, and **Tailwind CSS**. Fully localized in **Arabic (RTL)** and **English (LTR)** with multi-currency support and PWA offline capabilities.

---

## ✨ Features | المميزات الرئيسية

- 🔄 **Dual Investment Strategies | استراتيجيتان للاستثمار**:
  - **Monthly Recurring (`استثمار شهري دوري`)**: Simulate regular monthly contributions alongside an optional starting principal.
  - **Lump Sum (`مبلغ إجمالي دفعة واحدة`)**: Calculate returns for a one-time upfront investment with customizable compounding frequencies (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
- 🌍 **Full Bilingual & RTL Support | دعم كامل للغتين والعربية**:
  - Instant one-click toggle between Arabic (native RTL layout with Cairo & IBM Plex Sans fonts) and English (LTR).
- 💱 **Multi-Currency Engine | دعم متعدد العملات**:
  - Live currency formatting for **USD ($)**, **SAR (ر.س)**, **AED (د.إ)**, **KWD (د.ك)**, and **EUR (€)**.
- 📊 **Visual Growth Projection | رسوم بيانية تفاعلية**:
  - Interactive stacked charts powered by **Recharts** displaying principal invested vs. accumulated compound returns over time (Area and Bar modes).
- 📑 **Responsive Annual Breakdown Schedule | جدول التوزيع والنمو السنوي**:
  - Year-by-year financial ledger (Starting Balance, Annual Contributions, Interest Earned, Cumulative Returns, Ending Balance).
  - **One-click CSV Export**: Download the entire multi-year breakdown with proper UTF-8 BOM encoding for seamless opening in Microsoft Excel.
- 🎯 **Benchmark Quick Presets | مؤشرات العائد السنوي المرجعية**:
  - Fast selection presets for common portfolio targets: High-Yield Cash (4%), Balanced 60/40 (6.5%), S&P 500 Historical Average (10%), and Growth & Tech (12.5%).
- 🌓 **Dark & Light Mode | الوضع الليلي والنهاري**:
  - Sleek modern glassmorphism design with system preference detection and manual toggle.
